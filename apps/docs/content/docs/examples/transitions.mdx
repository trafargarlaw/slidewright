The headmatter gives the `fade` transition to every slide. A slide with its
own `transition` key uses that transition. The last slide uses `turn`, a
transition that the stylesheet makes.

- [Transitions](/docs/reference/syntax#transitions) in the deck syntax
  gives the names of the transitions.
- [Your own transitions](/docs/reference/react#your-own-transitions) tells
  how to make a transition with CSS.
