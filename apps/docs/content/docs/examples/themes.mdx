Click a theme to see the deck in it. The colour scheme of the site, light
or dark, is also the colour scheme of the deck.

<ThemesDeck deck="examples/themes/slides.md" />

| Theme      | Look                                                         | For                                      |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| `default`  | Neutral greys and indigo                                     | All decks                                |
| `paper`    | Warm paper and ink, serif headings                           | Talks that tell a story                  |
| `frost`    | Cool greys and teal, rounded corners, a glow on title slides | Technical talks                          |
| `contrast` | Black and white, bold headings, a contrast of 7:1 or more    | Bright rooms, and people with low vision |
| `vivid`    | Pink and violet, narrow headings, section slides in colour   | Keynotes                                 |

To use a theme, set `theme` in the headmatter, the frontmatter of the first
slide:

```md
---
theme: frost
---
```

Each theme has colours for light and for dark. The `colorScheme` key
selects one, and `auto` follows the system. To change a colour of the
theme, set its property in your stylesheet. Your rules win over the theme:

```css title="style.css"
[data-deck] {
  --deck-accent: light-dark(#0f766e, #5eead4);
}
```

See [Themes](/docs/reference/react#themes) for the properties, and for how
to make a theme with a name of its own.
