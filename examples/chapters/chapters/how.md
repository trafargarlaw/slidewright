# A slide with `src`

```md
---
src: chapters/how.md
class: how
---
```

It stands for the slides of that file. Its other keys, here `class`, go to
each of them: `style.css` colours the headings of `.how` slides.

---

# The settings of a chapter

```md
---
defaults:
  layout: center
---
```

`defaults` at the top of a chapter apply to its slides, so this slide is
centred. The theme, the colours and the size are those of `slides.md`.
